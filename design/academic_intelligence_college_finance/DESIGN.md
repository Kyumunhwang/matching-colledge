## Brand & Style

This design system serves a high-stakes, analytical counseling environment bridging affluent international school families, ambitious students, and senior academic directors. The visual tone balances elite institutional prestige with mathematical transparency. It rejects decorative fluff, consumer gamification, and hyperactive gradients in favor of an **editorial, data-dense, institutional modernism**.

The emotional target is calm clarity and rigorous authority. US college admissions—specifically for international applicants navigating complex visa classifications, merit matrices, and need-aware financial aid barriers—is fraught with anxiety and misinformation. The interface conveys total control: counsel-vetted models, clear financial trade-offs, and an explicit information firewall separating raw algorithmic draft scores from client-approved admission rosters.

Visual language highlights:
- **Structural Density**: Crisp micro-surfaces, fine hairline dividers, and systematic metric cards.
- **Editorial Legibility**: Humanist geometric sans typefaces paired with high-legibility tabular figures for financial transparency.
- **Controlled Chromatic Hierarchy**: Navy slate foundations, academic sapphire action anchors, and strict functional semantics for admissions risk (Reach / Match / Safety) and financial distributions.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around institutional gravitas, data visualization fidelity, and distinct semantic states.

### Core Functional Rules
- **Primary Navy (`#0F172A`)**: Represents institutional authority, critical typography, master navigation headers, and modal frame anchors.
- **Secondary Academic Sapphire (`#2563EB`)**: Drives all forward-moving system actions, active tabs, verified state selections, and interactive metric points.
- **Tertiary Aid Emerald (`#059669`)**: Strictly reserved for positive fiscal metrics: predicted merit awards, institutional aid packages, and full-need-met guarantees.
- **Alert Coral & Warning Amber (`#DC2626`, `#F59E0B`)**: Alert Coral signals out-of-pocket net deficits, admissions reach thresholds, and hard eligibility disqualifications. Amber signals financial caution, such as need-aware penalties and conditional eligibility requirements.

### Surface and Border Architecture
Surfaces transition from crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) on top of a low-strain ambient canvas (`#F8FAFC`). Hairlines in `#E2E8F0` segment complex data rows without visual clutter. High-stress elements (such as unapproved AI drafts) leverage `#1E293B` backing to visually signal gated internal review before publishing to client views.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines institutional gravitas with computational clarity. 

- **Display & Headings (Manrope)**: Chosen for its balanced geometric proportions and structured tone. High-level metric figures, student cohort rosters, and university headers convey executive presence without becoming flamboyant.
- **Body & Controls (Inter)**: Built for screen legibility, dynamic form tabs, evaluation notes, and high-density tables. Inter delivers consistent vertical rhythm across varied line lengths.
- **Data Tokens & Financials (JetBrains Mono)**: Applied to financial figures, currency conversions, standardized test percentiles, GPA inputs, schema keys, and status flags. Tabular alignment prevents layout shifting when financial aid sliders update in real time.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a multi-tier dashboard model, ensuring counselors can process complex multi-dimensional data while clients receive an uncluttered, clear experience.

### Grid Foundations
- **Admin Workspace (Counselor Screen)**: A dedicated 3-column asymmetric layout:
  - **Left Rail (Student Navigator)**: Fixed 280px to 320px sidebar for rapid student record search, batch tagging, and risk flags.
  - **Center Workspace (Data Entry & Modeling)**: Fluid central stage (min 600px) housing tabbed curriculum panels (IB DP / AP / GPA) and financial sliders.
  - **Right Panel (AI Control & Publication)**: Sticky 360px panel housing real-time match projections, counselor override actions, and publish confirmations.
- **Client & Parent Portal**: Fluid responsive layout capped at a max-width of 1440px. Match selections are arranged in a structured grid (3-column on desktop, 2-column on tablet, and single-column stacked on mobile).

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: Full multi-column view with independent vertical scroll containers for the data form and AI preview drawer.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: Admin navigation collapses into a slide-over sheet; university match rosters adjust to a 2-column layout; financial gauge displays collapse to vertical progress bars.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single-column flow with persistent top bar summary metrics (Total Grant vs Net Tuition) and a floating primary CTA for status confirmation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp borders, calibrated background tints, and soft institutional shadows rather than dramatic skeuomorphism.

### Depth Stratification
1. **Base Layer (Canvas)**: Slate tint (`#F8FAFC`). Pure utility surface.
2. **Structural Layer (Cards & Panels)**: True White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid hairline (`#E2E8F0`). Subtle drop shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
3. **Interactive & Focus Layer (Active Sliders & Selection)**: Elevation tier `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. High-priority cards (such as Reach/Safety alerts) feature a 2px left border accent.
4. **Overlay Layer (Publication Sheet & Approval Modals)**: Floats above an institutional backdrop wash (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.60)`) using deep elevation: `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

### The Information Firewall Indicator
To clearly separate draft AI calculations from counselor-verified data, unapproved cards use a micro-patterned background tint with an amber border. Once verified and published, the card transitions to a solid crisp white surface with an emerald-accented stamp of counselor approval.

## Shapes

The design uses a restrained, professional corner radius (`roundedness: 1`, baseline 4px / 0.25rem).

- **Badges, Tags, & Metric Pills**: Strict 4px radius. They convey data precision rather than playful pills.
- **Standard Cards, Sliders, & Text Fields**: 6px to 8px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), ensuring clean containment without soft, toy-like forms.
- **System Modals & Primary Panels**: 12px corner radius (`rounded-xl`), creating clear focal points for modal workflows.
- **Gauges & Dual-Track Progress Bars**: Clean flat-edge caps or subtle 2px rounded corners to preserve visual alignment against financial scale markers.

## Components

### 1. Match & Institutional Badges
- **Reach Badge**: Text `#B91C1C`, Background `#FEE2E2`, Border `#FCA5A5`. Uppercase tracking (`+0.04em`), font size 11px.
- **Match Badge**: Text `#1D4ED8`, Background `#DBEAFE`, Border `#93C5FD`.
- **Safety Badge**: Text `#047857`, Background `#D1FAE5`, Border `#6EE7B7`.
- **Need-Blind vs. Need-Aware Badges**: 
  - Need-Blind: Monospace text, teal background tint (`#CCFBF1`), teal border.
  - Need-Aware: Monospace text with alert symbol prefix, amber background tint (`#FEF3C7`), amber border (`#FCD34D`).

### 2. Financial Affordability Gauge
- **Dual-Fill Comparative Bar**: 
  - Height: 12px. Track background: `#F1F5F9`.
  - Left Segment (Predicted Aid): Rich Emerald `#059669`.
  - Right Segment (Net Family Out-of-Pocket): Vivid Coral `#DC2626`.
- **Legend & Metric Anchors**: Monospace currency figures stacked directly underneath each segment with clear percentage breakdowns.

### 3. Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Publish / Calculate)**: Solid Deep Navy (`#0F172A`) with white text. Focus outline: 2px ring in `#2563EB` with 2px offset. Hover: `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary Action (Counselor Override)**: Crisp white surface, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive / Reject Action**: White surface, border `#FCA5A5`, text `#DC2626`. Hover: `#FEF2F2`.

### 4. Form Controls & Sliders
- **Inputs & Dropdowns**: 1px solid border `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`. Inactive placeholders: `#94A3B8`. Active focus: Border `#2563EB`, subtle 1px ring `#93C5FD`.
- **Curriculum Dynamic Toggle**: Segmented control pill group. Inactive options: clear background, `#64748B` text. Active option: `#FFFFFF` card, subtle shadow, `#0F172A` bold text.
- **Financial Range Slider**: 
  - Track: 6px height `#E2E8F0`. 
  - Fill: Academic Sapphire `#2563EB`. 
  - Thumb: 20px circle in `#FFFFFF`, with 2px solid border `#2563EB` and subtle shadow.

### 5. University Matching Cards
- **Card Shell**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, padding 20px.
- **Header**: University seal/monogram alongside university name in `Manrope SemiBold`, paired with citizenship and major compatibility tags.
- **Metric Row**: 3-column micro-grid displaying Acceptance Rate, 5D Match Score (0–100 scale), and Expected Family Contribution (EFC) delta.
- **Footer**: Verification indicator showing counselor approval state and direct action to view the financial breakdown modal.
